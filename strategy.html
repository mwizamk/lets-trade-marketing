01 / STRATEGY

Complete 90-Day Zambia + Global Marketing Plan

The objective is not likes. It is a measurable funnel: traffic → leads → WhatsApp conversations → orders → payments → active subscriptions → renewals → referrals.

90-day benchmark targets

KPIDay 30Day 60Day 90
Organic clicks/month100+300+750+

These are planning benchmarks, not guaranteed results.

Phase 0 — Days 1–7: Foundation

  • Finish website, pricing, order and subscription workflow.
  • Install Google Analytics/Search Console and advertising conversion tracking.
  • Install Meta and TikTok measurement where supported.
  • Create UTM naming conventions.
  • Connect marketing events to your database.

Phase 1 — Days 8–30: Zambia

  • Launch core service and trust pages.
  • Publish one or two useful SEO articles per week.
  • Post short-form content consistently.
  • Build genuine reviews and local trust.
  • Drive social traffic into the website and WhatsApp.

Phase 2 — Days 31–60: Paid acquisition

  • Test Meta WhatsApp/lead campaigns.
  • Launch high-intent Google Search campaigns.
  • Retarget website and social engagers.
  • Keep only creatives and keywords that produce qualified leads and customers.
  • Begin renewal reminders.

Phase 3 — Days 61–90: Scale

  • Launch referral tracking.
  • Optimize customer retention and cross-selling.
  • Test one or two additional markets, starting with Zimbabwe and Malawi.
  • Use country-specific landing pages, pricing, payment options and terms.
  • Scale only campaigns with acceptable economics.

International expansion order

Start with Zambia. Then test Zimbabwe and Malawi. If economics work, evaluate Kenya, South Africa, Botswana, Namibia, Ghana and Nigeria individually. Never assume one country's CAC, payment behavior or conversion rate will transfer to another.
