Technical SEO checklist

  • Mobile-first responsive design
  • HTTPS
  • Fast loading and optimized images
  • Clean URLs
  • Sitemap.xml
  • Robots.txt
  • Canonical URLs where needed
  • Unique title/meta descriptions
  • Logical H1/H2 hierarchy
  • Internal links between related pages
  • Structured data only where appropriate and accurate
  • Google Search Console monitoring

Local SEO

If you genuinely serve specific locations, create useful local pages such as /lusaka/, /kitwe/, /ndola/, /chingola/ and /livingstone/. Each should contain genuinely local information. Do not mass-produce near-identical city pages.
