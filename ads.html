04 / ADVERTISING

Meta + Google + TikTok Advertising

Start small, measure completed business outcomes, then scale winners. Do not optimize for likes alone.

Meta Ads

Campaign 1 — Prospecting

Location: Zambia. Start broad enough to gather data. Objective: leads/messages or the platform's current equivalent. Use your strongest organic vertical video.

Campaign 2 — Retargeting

People who visited the website, engaged with Facebook/Instagram, watched videos or clicked into the funnel.

Campaign 3 — Existing customers

Renewal and cross-sell communication, subject to platform policies and your consent/marketing practices.

Google Search

Start with high-intent searches such as:

  • Netflix Zambia
  • Netflix subscription Zambia
  • Netflix price Zambia
  • Spotify Zambia
  • Spotify Premium Zambia
  • Prime Video Zambia
  • Showmax Zambia

Monitor actual search terms and add irrelevant terms to negative keyword lists where appropriate.
