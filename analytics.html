Core formulas

Website conversion: Orders ÷ Website Visitors × 100

Lead conversion: Customers ÷ Qualified Leads × 100

CAC: Advertising Spend ÷ New Customers

ROAS: Attributed Revenue ÷ Ad Spend

Renewal rate: Renewed Customers ÷ Customers Eligible for Renewal × 100

Churn: Customers Lost ÷ Customers at Start × 100

Weekly optimization

Every week review SEO, content, advertising, WhatsApp and business metrics. Then use the rule: Kill → Improve → Scale.
